# The display's page load and runtime cost Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Act on the page-load findings where they pay on the board, and measure what the layout and load changes do to the kiosk's CPU.

**Architecture:** Name the bundle and its assets by content, let lighttpd cache them for good and the page not at all, fetch the loading image early and stop its animation, and keep the auto refresh working.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin/JS, webpack config fragments in `webpack.config.d`, Gradle, lighttpd `mod_setenv`, pytest with testinfra.

**Spec:** [2026-10-03-page-load-design.md](../specs/2026-10-03-page-load-design.md)

## Global Constraints

- The auto refresh polls the ETags of the page and its scripts and reloads on a change.
- Hashing applies in production mode only.
- `index.html` keeps working in the development server with its plain names.

## Review Focus

- A build that finds no bundle or two loading images fails instead of writing a broken `index.html`.
- An upgrade replaces the hashed files and the open page reloads within a minute.
- The Kotlin code's utility classes stay styled.
